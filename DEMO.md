# The Film Room

AI-generated tutorials from real job site footage.

## The Tutorial

The AI finds two workers doing the same task — one expert, one struggling. It generates a video walkthrough that teaches the difference.

The expert's clip plays. At a key moment, the video pauses. A chat bubble appears:

> _"Watch his right hand — single downward motion. No adjustment. He trusts the mortar bed."_

Cut to the learner's clip. Same task. Pause:

> _"Three taps on a block that was already level. This isn't a skill problem — it's a confidence problem."_

The AI cross-references both clips, explains the root cause, and closes with a takeaway:

> _"The difference isn't speed — it's trust. Fix the mortar spread, and the tapping disappears."_

A superintendent shows this directly to the worker. A 5-minute coaching session, auto-generated from footage that already exists. No one had to write it. No one had to film a training video. The lesson was already there — the AI just found it.
