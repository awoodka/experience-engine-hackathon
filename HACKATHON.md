# UMD x Ironsite Startup Shell Hackathon

**Theme:** Spatial Intelligence in the Physical World
**Dates:** February 20 (6 PM kickoff) - February 22 (judging)
**Duration:** 36 hours

## Prizes

- 1st: $10,000
- 2nd: $5,000
- 3rd: $2,500

## Project Types

- **Product-based** - Polished demo solving a spatial intelligence problem. Judged on design, execution, and real-world impact.
- **Research-based** - Novel hypothesis/method with rigorous experiments and clear findings.

Both judged primarily on **novelty/creativity** and **quality of execution**.

## The Challenge

1. **Define a Problem** - Find a spatial task where current models fail. Use models like Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude Opus, or GPT5 to reveal their lack of spatial intelligence.
2. **Develop a Technique** - Prompt engineering, fine-tuning, inference-time compute, or other methods to improve spatial understanding.
3. **Showcase the Impact** - Demo on a real-world problem. Doesn't need to be perfect, just show augmented spatial intelligence.
