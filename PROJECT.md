# Experience Engine

**Team Brick & Morty** | UMD x Ironsite Startup Shell Hackathon 2026

## The Problem

Workers on construction sites can go years without learning a new skill — not because they don't want to, but because nobody's watching closely enough to tell them what to learn.

Meanwhile, the best workers on site carry knowledge that no manual has ever captured. After thousands of hours of repetition, they've developed micro-habits — the way they position a block, how they stage mortar before a run, the three-point level check they do without thinking. These small, unconscious techniques make them faster, safer, and more consistent than everyone around them. But they can't teach what they can't articulate, and they usually don't even know they're doing it.

So new workers figure it out alone. They make the same mistakes for months. They watch the experts move twice as fast and don't understand why. They lose confidence. Some never catch up.

The construction industry has a $36B/year productivity problem, and a huge part of it is this: **the best knowledge on a job site lives in muscle memory, and it dies when that worker goes home.**

## Why We Built This

We had a choice. We could use AI video analysis to build another safety compliance tool, another inventory tracker, another checkbox product that treats workers like problems to manage.

We chose to go directly to the worker and ask: **how do we make your day better?**

Feeling confident at your job is one of the best feelings in the world. Knowing what you're doing, getting faster at it, watching yourself improve — that changes how someone shows up every morning. We want every worker to have that feeling as fast as possible.

Experience Engine is built on a simple belief: if you can identify what makes someone great at their job, you can help everyone else get there too. Not through generic training videos. Through real moments from real footage — the specific habits, techniques, and micro-decisions that separate a 10-year mason from a 10-month mason.

**That's why we're the Experience Engine. We're engineering the experience of the worker — making them more confident, more capable, and more supported every single day.**

## What It Does

Experience Engine watches job site video and builds a searchable behavioral index — every action, every technique, every moment of expertise and every moment of struggle, tagged by timestamp and fully queryable by AI.

Then it connects the dots that no superintendent has time to make:

- **"This mason sets blocks in one motion with no adjustment. That mason re-taps blocks that are already level."** — Same trade, same task, completely different technique. One has muscle memory. The other is second-guessing. That's a teaching moment.

- **"This plumber executes press cycles smoothly and checks joint indicators every time. That one hesitates and re-measures after breaks."** — Confidence gap. Fixable with 5 minutes of mentorship from the right person.

- **"3 workers showed inefficiency in mortar staging today. Your fastest mason was on site."** — Tomorrow might be a good day for a quick demo.

The system doesn't just rank workers. It finds the _specific behaviors_ that make someone great, identifies who would benefit most from learning them, and gives superintendents the information they need to make it happen.

## How It Works

1. **Video In** — Helmet-mounted and site cameras capture raw job site footage.
2. **Behavioral Index** — AI (Google Gemini) analyzes every segment of video and extracts a structured timeline: activities, tools, materials, spatial context, expertise signals, inefficiency signals, ergonomic notes, communication events, and risk levels. Segments are 5-45 seconds of granularity.
3. **Search Engine** — An AI agent (Claude) queries the entire behavioral dataset through a conversational interface. Ask it anything — compare workers, find patterns, identify teaching opportunities.
4. **Actionable Insights** — The results are specific, timestamped, and backed by real footage. Not theory. Not guesses. Real moments from real work.

## What We Can Build With This

The search engine is the foundation. Here's where it goes:

- **Teaching Opportunities Feed** — Automatically surface mentorship matches: "Worker A demonstrates expert corner leads. Worker B struggles with the same technique. Connect them."
- **Skill Gap Reports** — Across all footage, which techniques have the biggest gap between best and worst? Ranked by impact.
- **Micro-Lesson Clips** — Auto-extract 30-second annotated clips from expert footage. Turn real work into real training.
- **Worker Growth Tracking** — Film the same workers over time. Watch inefficiency signals disappear and expertise signals emerge.
- **Superintendent Daily Digest** — "Here are today's coaching opportunities, ranked by who would benefit most."
