# Experience Engine

**Team Brick & Morty** | UMD x Ironsite Startup Shell Hackathon 2025

## The Problem

The best workers on a job site carry something that can't be written in a manual. After years of repetition, they develop expert micro-behaviors — small, subconscious habits that make them faster, safer, and more efficient than everyone around them. The way they position their body, how they stage their materials, the split-second glances they make before every action. They don't even know they're doing it. And because they can't articulate it, it never gets passed down. New workers are left to figure it out on their own, repeating the same mistakes for years.

## Our Solution

Experience Engine uses AI to watch workers, find the experts, and reverse-engineer what makes them great. We take raw job site video, break it down into a searchable behavioral timeline, and let an AI agent analyze every movement, habit, and spatial decision across the entire dataset. Ask it a question — *"What does the best mason on this site do differently?"* — and it finds the answer, backed by real moments from the footage. It's not a training video. It's a system that discovers what should be taught in the first place.

## How It Works

We pre-process construction video footage into a structured behavioral index — a searchable record of every action, spatial relationship, and worker behavior, tagged by timestamp. An AI agent queries this index to identify patterns that separate high-performers from everyone else, then surfaces those insights through a simple conversational interface. The spatial intelligence challenge is at the core: the AI has to understand what humans are doing in three-dimensional space from flat video to draw any meaningful conclusions.
